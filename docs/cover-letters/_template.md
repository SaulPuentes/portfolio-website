  Structure rules the build depends on:

  - Header (name, title, contact) is NOT written here, it comes from the CV data

---

Dear <Company> Hiring Team,

Opening: the role, years of experience, the stack overlap. One claim they can check.

Two or three proof paragraphs. Each one maps to something their posting asked for, named
company plus what you built plus the outcome. Nothing they cannot verify against the CV.

Closing: how you work, availability, one line asking for the conversation.

Best regards,

**<Your Name>**

---

## Notes before sending

- **Gaps not claimed** — list what you deliberately left out and the prepared answer if asked.
- **Optional lines** — relocation, availability, salary, anything to paste in per posting.
- Swap the greeting for a named hiring manager if the posting or LinkedIn reveals one.
